One more step: enable the extension

  1. Open System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions.
  2. In the Extensions section choose By Category (the switch in the “By App” view does not work on macOS 26).
  3. Click ⓘ next to File System Extensions, turn on NTFS (ntfs4mac) and click Done.

The NTFS4Mac app shows whether the extension is enabled. Then reconnect your NTFS disk, or choose NTFS in Disk Utility › Erase to format one.

If Apple's built-in read-only NTFS driver mounts a disk first, unmount it and mount it again from Disk Utility, or run mount -F -t ntfs4mac /dev/diskXsY /some/folder.

To uninstall, run scripts/uninstall.sh from the source repository, or delete the two items listed on the first page.
